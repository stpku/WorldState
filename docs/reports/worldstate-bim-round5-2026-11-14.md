# WorldState BIM Round 5 — actual run, 2026-11-14

**Result:** local experimental AI-assisted preview works for **three separate visible wall-segment candidates**, with assumed scale; these are not three verified wall instances. V0.2 remains **NOT RELEASE-READY**. No independent real-room accuracy or human-cost improvement has been established. Existing WorldState Core and the frozen RealRoom protocol were not changed.

## What was run

- CPU: 4 allocated cores, AMD EPYC 7K83; Linux x86-64, Python 3.12.3, OpenCV 4.10.0, ONNX Runtime 1.20.1, NumPy 1.26.4, pycolmap 4.2.1, IfcOpenShell 0.8.4. No GPU was used.
- UperNet/ConvNeXt-tiny ADE20K, [upstream model](https://huggingface.co/openmmlab/upernet-convnext-tiny) (MIT) and [third-party ONNX export](https://huggingface.co/JElvis/upernet-convnext-tiny-ade-onnx) (MIT tag), revision `a8e2807c3479bc617b37d8ed53e68660b3fa1ebb`, SHA-256 `fea729ab6c5d338bc79ad3d986b073e6faa12c97255eafd78a1f7145326e534e`. Conversion was not independently verified against the original checkpoint. Three real images ran at 2.90–2.92 s/image after model load; inference plus postprocessing, **not** user time. Inspect [overlays and JSON](../../examples/bim_eyeful_room/semantic/). The 3691 image has a door proposal; none of these results has labeled ground truth. Wall regions merge adjacent walls, Hough lines have duplicates and an endpoint may be an occlusion or image edge. Confidence values are uncalibrated. Cross-image object association, instance counting and occlusion analysis are NOT_IMPLEMENTED/UNKNOWN.
- 32 real [Eyeful Tower](https://github.com/facebookresearch/EyefulTower) `raf_emptyroom` same-camera photos, all public MIT data. COLMAP CPU feature extraction/matching/mapping took 4.60 s on the above allocation. 28/32 images registered; 446 sparse points; mean inlier reprojection error 0.755 px. No independently measured pose error, independent scale, wall completeness or physical dimension accuracy was measured. Self-calibration yielded focal length 374.14 px; this is an estimate. [Full candidate](../../examples/bim_eyeful_room/geometry/geometry-candidate.json).
- Semantic voting over the actual registered SfM tracks classified 247 sparse points as wall and 14 as floor. Sequential RANSAC produced three wall-surface hypotheses with 160, 40 and 15 points; the first two are nearly parallel and could be the same surface. Floor support did not reach the minimum of 15. Mean plane residual is not room reconstruction accuracy. Sparse sampling, semantic errors, unknown scale and lack of ground truth make the hypotheses unsuitable as verified walls. [Result](../../examples/bim_eyeful_room/geometry/eyeful-sfm-semantic.json).
- Studio now runs CPU multi-view SfM on 4–32 uploaded, equal-size photos after a same-camera and holdout-exclusion confirmation. It retains upload order, records all source hashes, rejects under-50% registration as insufficient and can apply the registered camera's focal estimate while leaving pitch and scale unresolved. In a [real browser run](../../examples/bim_eyeful_room/multiview-preview/browser-result.json), 28/32 images registered, 456 points were retained, mean reprojection error was 0.769 px, and the registered photo drove two AI wall-segment candidates and an editable IFC. The [IR](../../examples/bim_eyeful_room/multiview-preview/original.json) contains all 32 source image hashes and the geometry result; wall endpoints still use single-view backprojection and the 1 m scale is assumed. The first Studio attempt used hash-sorted filenames, registered only 5/32 and failed to register the target image; its [original output](../../examples/bim_eyeful_room/geometry/studio-first-ordering-failure.json) retains the older overly permissive status. The current provider marks fewer than 3 or fewer than half the images as insufficient; that heuristic is not a release threshold.
- Eyeful's published `cameras.json` documents its coordinate system in metres. A [diagnostic comparison](../../examples/bim_eyeful_room/geometry/correlated-pose-comparison.json) of 28 camera centers against those Metashape estimates, after fitting a similarity transform, gives leave-one-out center disagreement RMSE 0.0228 in the reference's stated metre units, and rotation median disagreement 0.243° after an all-camera fit. Both reconstructions use the same images and scale is fitted: this is **not independent accuracy, independent scale recovery, or an acceptance result**.
- A separate 16-photo SfM attempt near `19_DSC3691.jpg` registered only 2 images, with 32 points, and is recorded as [INSUFFICIENT_REGISTRATION](../../examples/bim_eyeful_room/geometry/threewall-failed-geometry-candidate.json). The target photo did not register. The 0.266 px reprojection value is not evidence of successful geometry or accuracy; the self-calibrated focal length also differs substantially from the 32-photo run.
- A real browser ran actual model inference, and Studio selected three distinct line candidates and their endpoints; the script confirmed them in one action. This produced and edited a [three-segment IFC](../../examples/bim_eyeful_room/README.md), with separate nodes and no fabricated wall connections. Only an SfM-estimated focal length from a different photo sequence with the same camera-name prefix is reused; the target photo was not registered. Wall coordinates use single-photo backprojection with assumed pitch, roll, height and **1 m placeholder scale**. This is not multi-view triangulation of wall corners, a confirmed wall-instance count, a completed room or a valid metric anchor. Automation was labeled `simulated`; no human corrections or labor were measured. Bonsai edited an assumed wall height and Studio read it back with GUIDs intact. IFC schema and shape checks passed for all three segments.
- A separate TUM browser regression confirmed that changing a physical endpoint invalidates its old scale, then used the same depth frame to recalculate and rebind the new endpoint distance. That is a same-sensor development check, not independent accuracy validation or a new room test.
- Core + product regression: `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 PYTHONPATH=. /tmp/worldstate-bim-reset-venv/bin/pytest -q tests products/worldstate_bim/tests` → **199 passed**. Fake provider tests verify API wiring and are labeled fake; the real model/browser run above is separate. IfcOpenShell emitted its existing `utcnow` deprecation warnings.

## Reproduce

Use an isolated local workspace and allow downloads only for these public, license-reviewed assets. Paths below are examples. The model is ~238 MB; photos are ~14 MB. No customer material is uploaded.

```bash
python3.12 -m venv /tmp/ws-round5-venv
/tmp/ws-round5-venv/bin/pip install -r benchmarks/ws_geometry/requirements-round2-cpu.txt
export PYTHONPATH=.
export OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1
/tmp/ws-round5-venv/bin/python benchmarks/ws_structure/fetch_upernet.py --output /tmp/ws-upernet.onnx
/tmp/ws-round5-venv/bin/python benchmarks/ws_geometry/fetch_eyeful.py --sfm-only --output /tmp/ws-eyeful-images
/tmp/ws-round5-venv/bin/python benchmarks/ws_geometry/run_generic_sfm.py --images /tmp/ws-eyeful-images --output /tmp/ws-eyeful-sfm-NEW
/tmp/ws-round5-venv/bin/python benchmarks/ws_geometry/fetch_eyeful.py --manifest benchmarks/ws_geometry/eyeful-threewall-manifest.json --output /tmp/ws-eyeful-three-images
/tmp/ws-round5-venv/bin/python benchmarks/ws_geometry/run_generic_sfm.py --images /tmp/ws-eyeful-three-images --output /tmp/ws-eyeful-three-sfm-NEW
curl -fL 'https://fb-baas-f32eacb9-8abb-11eb-b2b8-4857dd089e15.s3.amazonaws.com/EyefulTower/raf_emptyroom/cameras.json' -o /tmp/ws-eyeful-camera-reference.json
/tmp/ws-round5-venv/bin/python benchmarks/ws_geometry/compare_eyeful_poses.py --geometry /tmp/ws-eyeful-sfm-NEW/geometry-candidate.json --reference /tmp/ws-eyeful-camera-reference.json --output /tmp/ws-correlated-pose-comparison-NEW.json
/tmp/ws-round5-venv/bin/python benchmarks/ws_geometry/run_semantic_sfm.py --model /tmp/ws-upernet.onnx --images /tmp/ws-eyeful-images --sparse /tmp/ws-eyeful-sfm-NEW/sparse/0 --output /tmp/ws-semantic-sfm-NEW.json
/tmp/ws-round5-venv/bin/python benchmarks/ws_structure/run_semantic_vision.py --model /tmp/ws-upernet.onnx --output /tmp/ws-overlays-NEW /tmp/ws-eyeful-images/19_DSC0541.jpg /tmp/ws-eyeful-images/19_DSC2503.jpg
/tmp/ws-round5-venv/bin/python -m products.worldstate_bim.studio_server --workspace /tmp/ws-studio-NEW --vision-model /tmp/ws-upernet.onnx --port 8766
```

Open `http://127.0.0.1:8766/`, create a project and upload an authorized photo. Select **AI 分析当前照片** and review the overlay. In a new project, **接受自动墙段候选（最多3段）** selects nonduplicate line and endpoint proposals; explicitly confirm, inspect and delete or re-mark mistakes. Alternatively accept or reject individual candidates and assign wall/node IDs. Select the geometry path, supply and bind a scale to its *actual* physical endpoints, state camera/height/thickness assumptions, review, then generate IFC. The model does not automatically estimate an engineering-valid scale or full room. Studio can also reconstruct all uploaded same-camera photos with its multi-view button and use a registered focal estimate; the current room builder does not triangulate wall corners across views. Missing constraints require the user to add evidence or produce only a partial draft. If model weights are omitted, manual and rule-based modes still work.

The three-segment real-model browser test uses `benchmarks/ws_realroom/studio_ai_three_segments.cjs`; the full multi-view + AI Studio test uses `benchmarks/ws_realroom/studio_multiview_ai_browser.cjs` and Playwright; the three-segment arguments are `BASE PHOTO COLMAP_RESULT NEW_OUTPUT_DIR`; the multi-view arguments are `BASE IMAGE_DIR SELECTED_NAME NEW_OUTPUT_DIR`. Set `NODE_PATH` to a Playwright installation and optionally `CHROMIUM_PATH` to a Chromium binary. It intentionally uses an assumed 1 m scale and is **not** a measurement or acceptance run. The separate [TUM script](../../benchmarks/ws_realroom/studio_tum_browser.cjs) checks changed-endpoint invalidation and rebinds a newly calculated same-sensor reference. [External edit script](../../benchmarks/ws_bim/bonsai_parameter_edit.py) and [Studio sync script](../../benchmarks/ws_realroom/studio_sync_browser.cjs) are used for the recorded Bonsai operation.

Grant access only to authorized project evidence and the required controlled tools (`inspect_evidence`, `inspect_element`, `update_element`, `validate_geometry`, `compile_ifc`, `compare_revisions`, and where needed `sync_ifc`); do not grant publication or unrelated data access.
