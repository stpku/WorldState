# Real-photo AI-assisted IFC development example

**Experimental, partial and unverified.** A real CPU model segmented the MIT-licensed Eyeful Tower `raf_emptyroom` photograph `19_DSC3691.jpg`. Studio automatically selected three nonduplicate wall/floor-line proposals; a browser script confirmed them in one operation. The existing single-photo floor-backprojection path then generated [three separate editable IFC wall segments](three-segment-preview/original.ifc), with [IR and source evidence](three-segment-preview/original.json). These are distinct **candidate segments**, not three independently verified wall instances or a closed room. Their connections and the unobserved gaps remain unknown. The script did not provide fixed pixel coordinates, perform human annotation or measure human time; classifications are explicitly `simulated`.

The 1 m anchor, initial 2.5 m height, 0.12 m thickness, zero pitch and zero roll are **assumptions**, not measurements. Focal length came from SfM on different photos with the same camera-name prefix; calibration is unverified, and this photograph was not registered by that SfM. Thus the dimensions, camera pose, identities and completeness are not established. Endpoints may be occlusions or image borders rather than real corners. Do not use this draft for engineering or describe it as an independently measured room.

- Studio changed wall height from assumed 2.5 m to 2.6 m: [IFC](three-segment-preview/revised.ifc), [IR](three-segment-preview/revised.json). Blender 5.2.2 / Bonsai 0.9.0.post1 then changed it to 2.7 m in [external IFC](three-segment-preview/bonsai-edited.ifc); Studio synchronized that edit to [IR](three-segment-preview/bonsai-synced.json) and [IFC](three-segment-preview/bonsai-synced.ifc). The [Bonsai](three-segment-preview/bonsai-report.json) and [readback](three-segment-preview/bonsai-sync-report.json) records show wall GUID `3rr2_kGz9LB9UAC8uWPK1F` preserved.
- IfcOpenShell 0.8.4 parsed original, revised, Bonsai-edited and synchronized IFC4; each has three `IfcWall` objects, three generated geometric shapes, zero schema errors and the same wall GUIDs. This checks file/edit behavior, **not** dimensional or semantic accuracy. An earlier [two-wall trial](preview/) is retained.
- Real AI [overlays](semantic/) show wall, floor, ceiling and proposed boundaries in three real images; the third has a door-region prediction. Semantic regions are not wall instances, scores are uncalibrated, and opening dimensions and cross-photo associations are not estimated.
- In a separate [Studio multi-view run](multiview-preview/browser-result.json), 32 uploaded photographs yielded 28 registered cameras and 456 sparse points; the selected image was registered. Studio applied the focal estimate, ran the real vision provider, generated [two wall segments](multiview-preview/original.ifc), and saved a [revision](multiview-preview/revised.ifc). The [IR](multiview-preview/original.json) links the camera estimate to all 32 image hashes. Scale, pitch and height remain assumed; wall endpoints are **single-view**, not triangulated. An [initial ordering failure](geometry/studio-first-ordering-failure.json) registered only 5/32 images; upload order is now retained and registration below 50% is explicitly marked insufficient (a diagnostic rule, not an acceptance gate).
- Independent COLMAP SfM on 32 same-camera images registered 28 and generated [446 sparse points](geometry/points.ply), with a mean reprojection error of 0.755 px; its [geometry candidate](geometry/geometry-candidate.json) has unknown scale. [Semantic-track fusion](geometry/eyeful-sfm-semantic.json) produced three RANSAC surface hypotheses with 160, 40 and 15 supporting points; the first two are near-parallel and may be one surface. These are **not three verified walls**, nor a measure of wall accuracy. A separate 16-image attempt near the three-segment photo registered only 2 images and is explicitly [insufficient](geometry/threewall-failed-geometry-candidate.json). Neither SfM result directly triangulates preview wall coordinates; those use the single-photo path. An [additional pose comparison](geometry/correlated-pose-comparison.json) compares 28 cameras with another reconstruction of the **same** images, with fitted scale; it is not independent ground truth.
